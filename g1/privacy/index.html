Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

G1 Prep is a study app for the Ontario G1 knowledge test, made by Yogesh Patel. This policy explains what information the app uses, why it uses it, and the choices you have.

At a glance

  • No account needed. The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your study data stays on your device. Bookmarks, test results and settings are not sent to us.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking.
  • Anonymous usage counts help us improve the app. They are never used for advertising.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your language choice, bookmarked questions, practice test results and progress are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and deleting the app deletes them. All questions are built into the app, so you can study without an internet connection.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure these ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and an app-specific identifier (IDFV)
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address (not GPS)
  • Ad and usage data, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crashes and performance

Google uses this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and measure results. If you allow it, Google may also use it to show ads that are more relevant to you. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

G1 Premium removes all ads.

App analytics

To understand which features are useful and how G1 Premium is working, the app sends anonymous usage counts to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the European Union. For example, an event may record that the Premium screen was opened and from which feature, that a purchase or restore finished, or that a practice test was completed and passed. Each event also includes the app language and whether Premium is active.

These counts never include your name, email address, your answers or anything you type. They are not used for advertising and are not shared with advertisers. TelemetryDeck replaces the device identifier with a one-way hashed value before storing it, so the data is not linked to your identity. Read TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Purchases

G1 Premium is an optional one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks with Apple whether Premium is active on your device, including when you tap Restore Purchases.

As described under App analytics, the app records anonymous events such as a completed purchase or restore. These events are used only to understand how the app is used.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for your permission before using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Settings option in the app.
  • Remove ads: G1 Premium removes all ads from the app.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for a driving test, who must be at least 16 years old to take the G1. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Information handled by the app is protected using standard industry measures. However, no method of transmitting or storing data over the internet is completely secure.
